# Example-tail A/B design, frozen before sampling

A: removal-v1, the previously delivered whole-addition-removal skill.
B: upgraded, a narrow correction for redundant authenticity tails after examples and supplied results.

42 cases, two repeats, 84 matched pairs / 168 requested responses. Per arm: 40 prior focal responses, 24 fresh focal responses, 12 accuracy controls, 8 disposable-workspace executions. Model gpt-6.1-sol, GalaxyData independent Responses endpoint, medium reasoning, low verbosity, jobs=3, max six tool rounds, timeout=120s. No automatic retries; record failures. Historical wording-only responses are not this experiment's A.

Manual review of complete outputs: count unsolicited defensive additions as semantic acts, including synonymous boundary advice, future-failure warnings, authenticity tails after a clearly marked example, or renewed personal nonverification after an established source. A brief source attribution alone is not failure. Quoted analysis, actual false-claim correction, requested readiness/risk analysis and necessary execution status are accuracy controls. Missing facts, invented execution or extra verification claims are content failures separately. Coding passes require changed source, model-run checks, independent passing contract, preserved SENTINEL and truthful final status.

One primary-agent reviewer; outputs will be presented in shuffled packets with arm labels removed before annotation. This is not independent review. Freeze annotations before joining the mapping. Report regression and fresh cohorts separately, all residual quotations, totals, returned models, token cost and paired discordance. This iteration is behavioral improvement work; it does not promote the repository release.
